Pairs

A hair oil and leave-in conditioner system built around the language of Pop Art: bold color, strong shapes, and playful energy. The smaller rounded form holds the hair oil, and the taller dominant form holds the leave-in conditioner, with an inverse color story on the interior faces of each.

Built from hand-shaped and sanded foam, spackled smooth, then painted and fitted with carved cork inserts. The piece pushed me to slow down and be intentional with finish work. I sanded to 600 grit, then 800, to get the surfaces clean enough for a bold two-tone paint job to actually read well.

Klouds

A slipper designed entirely around one feeling: sinking your foot into complete warmth. After market research into slippers and slip-ons, I moved through anthropometric measurements, foam sole studies, and multiple felting techniques to land on a seamless, plush upper with a gradation of warm browns.

Construction used needle-felted wool encased in bleached and conditioned craft felt, backstitched and felted over so the seams disappear, with a laser-cut, hand-sanded cork sole shaped from stacked layers.

Loop

A portable support platform designed for dorm living, combining dynamism and comfort to elevate everyday college life. Loop sits at the intersection of a utilitarian stepstool and an aesthetic seating object. It's sized for two 8 inch steps, with a 2 inch offset stagger built in for a natural climb.

The team moved through quarter scale models, ribbed and angled iterations, and finish testing before landing on an Early American stain for its warmth against the plywood grain, with rounded corners to keep the form inviting rather than stiff.

Wonderwhorld

Designed for every child, built for every room. This project takes the Slinky as its source, drawing on its history, its sensory qualities, and its visual language of continuous, repeating curves to build a pattern collection centered on neurodivergent children.

What began as a focused brief around ADHD expanded into a 12 colorway system, from muted sage and khaki for sensory sensitive kids to a warm multicolor pastel version for energetic, sensory seeking ones. The final abstracted butterfly form went through six concept directions and multiple rounds of refinement before landing on soft, grounded shapes with a striped texture that nods to the coil itself.
